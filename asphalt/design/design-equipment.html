Laboratory Layout Recommendations
▸ Compaction area: Isolated vibration zone, level concrete floor
▸ Conditioning bath: Adjacent to loading frame, minimal transport distance
▸ Sample prep: Clean area with mixer, oven, scales within 5m
▸ Data station: PC with test software, UPS backup
▸ Safety: Emergency stops on all powered equipment, PPE stations
▸ Calibration: Annual NIST-traceable for load cell, flow meter, thermometers
