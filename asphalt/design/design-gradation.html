Restricted Zone Avoidance
  • Restricted zone: Sieve sizes between control points where gradation should not pass through
  • Superpave 12.5mm: Avoid passing through 9.5mm restricted zone (max 90% passing)
  • Superpave 19mm: Avoid passing through 12.5mm restricted zone (max 90% passing)
  • Marshall: Controlled points act similarly — max 90% at adjacent sieve
  • Passing through restricted zone → tender mix, compaction problems
  • Use 0.45 power chart to visualize and verify compliance
  • Blend 3+ aggregate sources to achieve target gradation curve
