Mix Design Procedure
  • Select aggregate sources and perform blend gradation (ASTM D3515)
  • Determine design traffic level (ESALs) and select Ndesign gyrations
  • Prepare trial blends at 3-4 binder contents (0.5% increments)
  • Compact gyratory specimens to Ninitial, Ndesign, Nmax
  • Measure Gmm (Rice) and Gmb (bulk) for each blend
  • Plot volumetric curves and select optimum binder content at 4% Va
  • Verify all criteria at optimum: VMA, VFB, DP, stability, moisture
  • Run TSR (AASHTO T283) - minimum 80% retained strength
