Standard 4-inch diameter specimen for Marshall stability and flow testing

101.6 mm (4.00 in)

Target Air Voids

VMA (Min)
> 13%
VFB Range
65% - 78%
Key Notes
  • Specimens prepared at 4 bitumen contents (typically 4.5%, 5.0%, 5.5%, 6.0%)
  • 3 specimens per bitumen content for statistical validity
  • Compaction temperature: 135-145°C (275-295°F)
  • Condition in 60°C water bath for 30-40 min before testing
  • Extrude carefully to avoid specimen damage
  • Measure bulk specific gravity (Gmb) per ASTM D2726
