Key Formulas
Stability = Pmax × Cthickness
Flow = Deformation at Pmax (0.25 mm units)
MQ = Stability / Flow (kN/mm or lbf/0.01in)
Air Voids = 100 × (1 - Gmb / Gmm)
VMA = 100 - (Gmb × Ps / Gsb)
VFB = VMA - Air Voids
Pmax = maximum load • Cthickness = thickness correction factor (1.0 for 63.5mm) • Gmb = bulk specific gravity • Gmm = theoretical max specific gravity • Ps = aggregate % by weight • Gsb = aggregate bulk specific gravity
