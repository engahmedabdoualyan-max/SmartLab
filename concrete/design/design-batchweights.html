📚 Batching Standards Reference

ASTM C94 / C94M
Ready-Mixed Concrete. Batching tolerances: Cement ±1%, Aggregates ±2%, Water ±1%, Admixtures ±3%. Slump, air, temperature at discharge. Delivery time limits (90 min / 300 rev).
NRMCA QC Manual
National Ready Mixed Concrete Association Quality Control Manual. Plant certification, mixer calibration, moisture probes, batch recording, delivery tickets.
ASTM C138
Density (Unit Weight), Yield, and Air Content (Gravimetric). Verify yield matches design. Calculate actual w/c from batch weights.
ASTM C566
Total Moisture Content of Aggregate by Drying. Required for moisture correction. Sample at batching, dry to constant mass.
ASTM C70 / C127 / C128
Aggregate Specific Gravity & Absorption (Coarse C127, Fine C128). Surface moisture (C70). Required for SSD to oven-dry conversion.
EN 206 / BS 8500
European standard. Conformity criteria, assessment of compressive strength, identity testing, factory production control.
ECP 203 / ECP 204
Egyptian concrete production & QC. Local batching tolerances, cement certification, aggregate source approval, admixture approved list.
