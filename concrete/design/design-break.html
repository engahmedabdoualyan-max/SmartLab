🌡️ Nurse-Saul Method
M = Σ(T - T₀)Δt. Simple time-temperature summation. T₀ = -10°C for Type I cement. Best for normal concrete.
⚛️ Arrhenius Method
te = Σexp[E/R(1/T₀ - 1/T)]Δt. More accurate for varying temperatures. E = 33,500 J/mol for Type I.
📊 Strength-Maturity Curve
Log(S) = a + b·log(M). Calibrate with 3+ cylinder breaks at different ages. Track in-place strength real-time.
