📝 Standards
  • ASTM C31: Standard Practice for Making and Curing Concrete Test Specimens in the Field
  • ASTM C192: Standard Practice for Making and Curing Concrete Test Specimens in the Laboratory
  • Minimum 3 cylinders per test age for acceptance testing
  • L/D ratio correction factor applies for non-standard aspect ratios
  • Field-cured cylinders: place adjacent to structure at same temperature
