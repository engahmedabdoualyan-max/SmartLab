📝 Standards
  • ASTM C138: Standard Test Method for Density, Yield, and Air Content of Concrete
  • EN 12350-6: Testing fresh concrete - Density
  • Unit weight = (full weight - empty weight) / container volume
  • Typical range: 2200 - 2400 kg/m³ for normal-weight concrete
  • Yield = theoretical density / measured density; target 0.98 - 1.03
