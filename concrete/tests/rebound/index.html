Enter 12 rebound numbers (comma-separated) to estimate compressive strength.
📝 Standards
  • ASTM C805: Standard Test Method for Rebound Number of Hardened Concrete
  • EN 12504-2: Non-destructive determination of rebound number
  • Take 12 readings, discard outliers >15% from mean, average remaining
  • Correlation is approximate; calibrate against core tests for specific mixes
  • Do not use on: green concrete (<3 days), carbonated surfaces, or near reinforcement
