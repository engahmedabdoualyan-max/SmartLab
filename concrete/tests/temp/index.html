📝 Standards
  • ASTM C1064: Standard Test Method for Temperature of Freshly Mixed Hydraulic-Cement Concrete
  • EN 12350-4: Testing fresh concrete - Temperature
  • Acceptable range: 10°C to 30°C (typical specification)
  • Thermometer stem must be immersed at least 50 mm into concrete
  • High temperature (>30°C) accelerates setting and reduces ultimate strength
