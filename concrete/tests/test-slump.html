📝 Standards
  • ASTM C143: Standard Test Method for Slump of Hydraulic-Cement Concrete
  • EN 12350-2: Testing fresh concrete - Slump test
  • Slump classes: S1 (10-40 mm), S2 (50-90), S3 (100-150), S4 (160-210), S5 (≥220)
  • Shear or collapse slumps indicate segregation; test should be repeated
  • Cone lift: 15-75 mm vertical lift in 5-10 seconds
