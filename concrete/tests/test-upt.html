📝 Standards
  • BS 1881-203: Testing concrete - Recommendations for measurement of velocity of ultrasonic pulses
  • ASTM C597: Standard Test Method for Pulse Velocity Through Concrete
  • Velocity ranges: >4.5 km/s Excellent; 3.5-4.5 Good; 3.0-3.5 Fair; <3.0 Poor
  • Direct transmission gives most reliable results; indirect method for surface assessment
  • Temperature correction: +1% velocity per 10°C above 20°C calibration temperature
