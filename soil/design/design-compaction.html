📈 Standard vs Modified Curves
📊 Energy Comparison
Standard (T 99/D698): 5.5 lb hammer, 12" drop, 3 layers, 25 blows/layer = 12,375 ft-lbf/ft³
Modified (T 180/D1557): 10 lb hammer, 18" drop, 5 layers, 25 blows/layer = 56,250 ft-lbf/ft³
Ratio: Modified energy ≈ 4.5 × Standard
📈 Typical Differences
MDD (Modified) ≈ MDD (Standard) + 10-15 pcf
OMC (Modified) ≈ OMC (Standard) - 2-3%
Higher energy → denser packing at lower moisture
🛣️ Application
Standard: Light traffic, embankments, trench backfill
Modified: Heavy traffic, runways, highways, foundations
Use modified when compaction equipment delivers high energy

📋 Acceptance Criteria
AASHTO / ASTM: ≥ 95% Standard Proctor (embankments)
AASHTO / ASTM: ≥ 95% Modified Proctor (subgrade/base)
Moisture: Typically OMC ± 2% (or ± 3% for cohesive)
Nuclear (D2922/D3017): Direct wet/dry density + moisture
🔬 Test Methods
Nuclear: AASHTO T 310 / ASTM D6938 (fast, non-destructive)
Sand Cone: AASHTO T 191 / ASTM D1556 (reference)
Rubber Balloon: AASHTO T 205 / ASTM D2167
Drive Cylinder: AASHTO T 204 / ASTM D2937
