📝 Standards
  • Standard Proctor: 4.5kg hammer, 305mm drop height, 1000cm³ mold (ASTM D698)
  • Modified Proctor: 4.5kg hammer, 457mm drop height, 1000cm³ mold (ASTM D1557)
  • Typical test points: 5-15% moisture content in 2% increments
  • Optimum moisture: peak of dry density vs moisture curve
