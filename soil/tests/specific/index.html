📝 ASTM D854 Method
  • Specific Gravity Gs = W oven-dry / (W SSD - W immersed) × ρ_water
  • Water absorption = (W SSD - W oven-dry) / W oven-dry × 100
  • Temperature correction factor: 1 + 0.000009 × (T - 20)
  • Rapidly determine aggregate properties for mix design
