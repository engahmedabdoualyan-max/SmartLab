📝 ASTM D4318 Method
  • Liquid Limit: Moisture content where soil flows 13mm in 25 blows
  • Plastic Limit: Lowest moisture where soil can be rolled without cracking
  • Plasticity Index = LL - PL
  • LL range controls volumetric changes in fine-grained soils
