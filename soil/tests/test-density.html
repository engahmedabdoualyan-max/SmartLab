📝 ASTM D1556 Method
  • Sand cone method: excavate hole, fill with known volume of sand
  • Dry density = Field wet density × (100 / (100 + moisture%))
  • Target compaction: 95% of Standard Proctor for most applications
  • Minimum 5 tests per 200 m² for quality control
