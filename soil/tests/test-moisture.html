📝 ASTM D2218 Method
  • Standard oven: 105-110°C for 24 hours to constant weight
  • Rapid methods (IR, microwave) require correlation to oven method
  • Moisture content = (Wet weight - Dry weight) / Dry weight × 100%
  • Typical range: 5-40% for most soils, up to 100%+ for organic/clay soils
